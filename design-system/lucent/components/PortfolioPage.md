# PortfolioPage

The whole portfolio composed from the kit, in the order a reviewer needs it: the page hero (who, what, where, available), Projects (one featured card and two beside it, filterable), Experience (a scannable list that opens row by row), Drawings (the gallery with its lightbox), and Contact tiles. The glass nav follows the section in view and tightens on the way down.

Consumer provides the copy, project screenshots, drawings, links and resume. Section rhythm is `space-9`; side margin `space-8` on desktop, `space-5` on phones. The reasoning behind each section is in the Showcase section of the brand book.
